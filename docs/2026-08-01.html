Coldcard wallet flaw drains 594 BTC ($38M) from 500 wallets in a 25-minute sweep

A firmware bug introduced in Coldcard Mk3 devices in March 2021 caused seed generation to fall back from the hardware random-number generator to a predictable software substitute seeded by non-secret chip data. An attacker exploited the five-year-old flaw between 01:31 and 01:56 UTC Thursday, sweeping about 594 BTC from roughly 500 wallets; Coinkite says Mk4, Q, and Mk5 devices are unaffected.

why it mattersOne of the largest self-custody hardware-wallet losses on record, and a reminder that 'not your keys, not your coins' cuts both ways.
