AI

Anthropic told the SEC it may imminently meet its Responsible Scaling Policy's threshold for AI-accelerated AI development, and raised its disclosed compute commitments to $895.4 billion

The amended S-1 filed on 2 November says internal estimates for future models, not current ones, indicate Anthropic may imminently meet its policy's definition of dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI, that it does not expect to be able to safely let Claude design and build successive frontier models without meaningful human supervision, and that over 90% of contributions to its codebase in September were written by Claude. Purchase commitments, substantially all for compute, total about $895.4 billion, up from about $430 billion in the first filing on 26 October, and revenue grew 1,127% year over year in the third quarter.

why it mattersThe company behind your main coding tool now says, in a filing it is liable for, that its next models may be able to drive their own development, so expect a rewritten safety policy and possible new limits on how its most capable models are released.
