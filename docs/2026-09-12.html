Two more AI safety researchers quit Anthropic and Google DeepMind, warn 'no one is coming to save us'

Joe Benton, who led a safety research team at Anthropic, and Josh Engels, formerly of Google DeepMind, left their posts and are joining AI-incident nonprofit METR, days after ex-Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon's viral resignation post reignited Washington's AI-safety debate.

Claude Code ships plugin eval command and mid-run output-style switching

Anthropic's latest Claude Code release adds a `claude plugin eval` command that scores a plugin's test suite and returns reproducible JSON/HTML reports, plus `/output-style` for switching styles mid-session across CLI, VS Code, web, and Slack.

DeepSeek launches V4.1-Flash, says it beats its own larger V4-Pro flagship

DeepSeek's new 552B-parameter mixture-of-experts model adds native multimodal vision and a redesigned encoder-decoder architecture; the company says independent tests put it ahead of V4-Pro on performance, cost and speed, and will reroute all V4-Pro API traffic to it starting September 14.

Why it matters — A smaller model beating its own flagship undercuts the assumption that bigger is always better.

Sakana AI ships Fugu Ultra v2.0, pointedly excludes Claude and GPT-6 Astra from its model pool

Sakana's multi-agent orchestration model routes queries across a pool of underlying models behind one API; version 2.0 drops Claude Fable 5/5.1 and GPT-6 Astra from that pool while still leading published benchmarks like Chartography and DeepSWE.

Why it matters — Sakana is betting customers care more about not depending on one proprietary frontier model than about using the single best one.

Bitcoin

Blockstream refuses ransom for the last 600 BTC stolen in the Liquid Network exploit

After a bug in Liquid's Elements software let an attacker mint roughly 4,000 BTC of unbacked LBTC and peg it out on September 6, the attacker returned about 3,400 BTC but is holding the remaining ~598.5 BTC (~$47M) for a 10% bounty. Blockstream says it won't pay, calling the demand theft, and plans to work with law enforcement and forensic firms to trace the funds.

Why it matters — A hard line against paying hackers could set precedent for how other protocols handle post-hack extortion.

Bitcoin ETFs post worst single-day outflow since July as three-day pullback hits $449M

US spot Bitcoin ETFs shed $282.6M on Thursday, led by ARK 21Shares' ARKB ($164M), Grayscale's GBTC ($36M) and Fidelity's FBTC ($33.6M), bringing the three-day outflow total to $449M even as cumulative net inflows remain above $55B.
