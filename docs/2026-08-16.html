SpaceX finalizes $60 billion acquisition of Cursor maker Anysphere

SpaceX closed its all-stock purchase of Anysphere, the startup behind the widely used Cursor code editor, folding it into a new SpaceXAI division alongside xAI's Grok. It's the largest startup acquisition on record.

Why it matters — Consolidates one of the most popular AI coding tools inside SpaceX/xAI, reshaping the competitive map against Anthropic, OpenAI and Microsoft-backed rivals.

AI for Software Development

Grok Bot goes live in Cursor as developers flag vendor lock-in

Days after the deal closed, SpaceXAI's always-on 'Grok Bot' agents — which sign into a user's own apps and keep working in the background — rolled out to Cursor's paying tiers. Commentators note the acquisition leaves few independent, privacy-neutral AI coding tools above hobbyist scale.

Why it matters — Code written in Cursor now feeds a model stack owned by SpaceX — a real shift in who controls the data behind one of the most-used coding tools.

Alibaba's Qwen releases open-weight Qwen3.8-27B under Apache 2.0

The 27-billion-parameter multimodal dense model ships with a 262K native context window (extendable to 1M) and, per Qwen, beats the larger Qwen3.7-Plus on coding and office tasks while running on a single consumer GPU.

Why it matters — Another capable, permissively licensed open-weight model keeps pricing pressure on proprietary labs, extending a strong run of releases from Chinese labs this month.

Unpatched GeoServer zero-day under active exploitation

A SQL-injection flaw in GeoServer's PostGIS datastore filter handling (CVSS 9.8) can lead to remote code execution; attackers began probing within hours of public disclosure. Fixed versions — 3.0.1, 2.28.5 and 2.27.6 — are now available.

Why it matters — GeoServer has a history of being exploited at scale once a flaw goes public — teams running it should patch now.

The Hacker News

Grok 4.6 rolls out in GitHub Copilot

xAI's latest reasoning model is now available across Copilot's Pro, Pro+, Max, Business and Enterprise tiers, positioned for agentic coding and complex multi-step workflows.

Why it matters — Widens model choice inside the most widely used coding assistant, days after Grok 4.6's standalone launch.

GitHub Changelog

GitHub adds multiple redirect URIs and token refresh for OAuth and GitHub Apps

App developers can now register multiple or wildcard redirect URIs and opt into expiring access tokens paired with six-month refresh tokens, replacing indefinitely valid tokens for apps that adopt the new flow.

Why it matters — Expiring tokens close off a long-standing weak point for any integration whose long-lived token leaks.

Bitcoin

Cboe files for the first US 3x-leveraged Bitcoin and Ether ETFs

Cboe BZX submitted a rule-change application, sponsored by Volatility Shares, for triple-leveraged BTC and ETH products structured as CFTC-regulated commodity pools holding CME/COMEX futures rather than spot assets. The SEC opened the filing for public comment on August 14.

Why it matters — Would be the most leveraged regulated bitcoin exposure available in the US if approved, though no listing date has been set.

The Block

Trump expected at White House meeting with crypto CEOs and SEC, CFTC chiefs

Trump is expected to attend a session with executives from Coinbase, Ripple, Robinhood, Gemini, Polymarket and Kalshi alongside CFTC chair Michael Selig and SEC chair Paul Atkins, kicking off the CFTC's new Innovation Advisory Committee.

Why it matters — Sets the stage for possible regulatory movement after the SEC abruptly postponed its Reg Crypto vote and the CLARITY Act stalled in the Senate.

CoinDesk

Binance to block HTX and 10 other platforms over EU Russia sanctions

Binance began winding down transaction processing with 16 platforms in a phased schedule, with HTX, EXMO and nine others cut off from August 23 to comply with the EU's Russia sanctions package. It's not a delisting, but transfers to or from the affected platforms trigger a compliance hold.

Why it matters — HTX is a major exchange; liquidity providers are already pulling back from its books ahead of the cutoff, a sign of the disruption sanctions enforcement can cause even short of a formal delisting.
