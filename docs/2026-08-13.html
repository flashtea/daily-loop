Google's Gemini app hits 1 billion monthly users

Google said the Gemini app now has more than 1 billion monthly active users, up from 950 million in July, making it the company's fastest-growing product ever and the 14th Google product to cross the billion-user mark.

Why it matters — Gemini is now growing about as fast as ChatGPT, which crossed the same threshold in June, sharpening the race for default-AI-assistant status.

Lovable raises $400M at a $13.3B valuation, doubling in six months

AI app-building platform Lovable closed a $400M Series C led by Menlo Ventures and the EU's Scaleup Europe Fund, taking its valuation to $13.3B just six months after a $6.6B Series B, with Tencent among the new backers.

Why it matters — Lovable says it now hosts 60 million projects and hit $500M in annualized revenue in June, a sign 'vibe coding' platforms are scaling as fast as dedicated coding agents.

Cognition reportedly in talks to raise at a $40B valuation

Cognition, maker of the Devin coding agent, is reportedly in early talks for a new funding round that could value the company at $40B or more, up from the $26B valuation it set in a $1B raise this past May, according to Bloomberg.

Why it matters — Cognition says enterprise usage of Devin has grown 50% month-over-month for six straight months, on a track toward $1B in annualized revenue.

AI Research & Models

Meta releases Muse Glimmer, a 30B open-weight model built for local agents

Meta's Superintelligence Labs open-sourced Muse Glimmer under an Apache 2.0 license: a 30-billion-parameter model tuned for tool use and long-horizon agent tasks that runs quantized on a single consumer GPU or Mac, with no cloud dependency.

Why it matters — It's Meta's most concrete open-weight release since the Llama line stalled, targeting the always-on local-agent niche that labs like Nvidia are also chasing.

TechCrunch

OpenAI launches GPT-5.6-Cyber and splits its Daybreak program into two access tiers

OpenAI released GPT-5.6-Cyber, built on GPT-5.6 Sol for exploit validation and vulnerability research, and expanded its Daybreak security-partner program into a guardrail-reduced 'Blue' tier and a 'Red' tier with full model access for vetted defenders like CrowdStrike and Cisco.

Why it matters — The model reached the 'High' cyber-capability threshold under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework; access requires identity verification and mandatory hardware security keys starting September 1.

Bitcoin

Bitcoin slips toward $63,500 as traders look past in-line CPI to the Fed's next tests

Bitcoin eased to about $63,500 after July's US inflation print matched forecasts, trimming futures-market odds of a September Fed rate cut to roughly 38% from 46% and failing to spark the rally some traders had hoped for.

Why it matters — The muted reaction confirms Bitcoin's price is currently tracking Fed-rate expectations more closely than crypto-specific catalysts.

CoinDesk

Metaplanet moves 3,881 BTC between wallets as its paper loss nears $1.4B

Japanese treasury firm Metaplanet shifted about $247M worth of Bitcoin between wallets it controls over a three-hour window; blockchain data shows the funds did not move to an exchange, so it isn't a confirmed sale, but it comes as the firm sits on a roughly 34% unrealized loss on its 43,000 BTC holdings.

Why it matters — Metaplanet is one of the largest corporate BTC holders outside the US; a transfer this size, even between its own wallets, is being watched closely given its mounting paper losses.
