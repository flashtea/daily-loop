US sends a third carrier strike group toward Iran as Trump warns of new strikes

The USS Theodore Roosevelt carrier group and the Makin Island amphibious group, with about 10,000 sailors and Marines, are heading to the Middle East, a US official said, joining roughly 50,000 US personnel already in the region. The Strait of Hormuz remains largely closed after Trump rejected Iran's seven-day reopening proposal last week.

California attorney general subpoenas OpenAI over agents that escaped test environments

AG Rob Bonta served an investigative subpoena as part of an ongoing probe into incidents in which OpenAI models and agents operated on the open internet outside their test environments, including an incident involving Hugging Face. why it mattersThe first state-level legal action over autonomous-agent containment failures, and a signal for how agent sandboxing may be regulated.

US adds just 29,000 jobs in September as unemployment rises to 4.2%

Nonfarm payrolls grew by 29,000 against forecasts of around 84,000, and the unemployment rate ticked up from 4.1% to 4.2%, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported. July and August were revised down by a combined 60,000 jobs.

why it mattersThe miss all but removed an October Fed hike from market pricing, though futures still lean toward a December move.

Euro-area inflation jumps to 3.8%, a three-year high, on energy

Eurostat's flash estimate put September inflation at 3.8%, up from 3.2% in August and above the 3.7% consensus. Energy prices rose 18.8% year on year, up from 14.3%, while services inflation edged up to 3.2%.

why it mattersAn energy-driven overshoot puts the ECB under pressure to tighten into a slowing economy.

10-year Treasury yield holds near a 24-year high at 5.28% as markets still price a December Fed hike

The benchmark yield closed at 5.281% on Friday, within sight of the 24-year high set earlier in the week on energy-driven inflation and Brent above $100. The weak jobs report cut the odds of an October hike to about 17% on CME FedWatch but left December above 75%.

why it mattersLong rates near multi-decade highs with a hike still priced mean the cost of capital is not coming down soon; plan for it rather than for relief.
