Amodei calls for AI labs to slow capability gains; Altman and Musk quickly back him

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay urging AI companies to deliberately slow the pace at which they improve frontier model capabilities, warning that within 6-12 months a swarm of rogue AI agents could be capable of taking over the internet with a persistent botnet. OpenAI's Sam Altman and xAI's Elon Musk both endorsed the call within hours, and OpenAI separately ruled out an IPO this year, citing safety concerns.

why it mattersThree fiercely competing CEOs publicly agreeing to slow down is a rare, concrete signal that the labs see acute near-term risk, not just PR.

Anthropic's promotional 50% increase to Claude Code's weekly usage limits ended September 13; the permanent replacement raising standard limits by 25% for Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans took effect today, which the company itself acknowledges works out to roughly 17% less weekly usage than subscribers had during the promo.

Gemini CLI 0.59.0 closes an SSRF hole and enforces fail-closed workspace trust

Google's Gemini CLI shipped version 0.59.0 this week with two security fixes: it now prevents server-side request forgery during MCP OAuth metadata discovery, and restricted mode enforces a fail-closed workspace-trust policy that filters which MCP servers can run.

why it mattersCloses a real remote-exploit path in how the CLI talks to MCP servers, not just a UX bug.

Claude Code 2.1.268 closes two more permission-check bypasses

The latest Claude Code release fixes deny/ask rules that failed to apply on symlinked directories and on commands wrapped in env -C or eval, and stops the /mcp and claude mcp list commands from printing secrets resolved from ${VAR} placeholders.
