Claude Produces First Complete, Machine-Checked Proof of Fermat's Last Theorem

Anthropic says dozens of Claude agents working largely autonomously over 11 days on the open Prove2Me platform produced the first end-to-end, computer-verified proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in the Lean 4 proof language, writing about 13 million lines of Lean and proving roughly 29,500 supporting theorems while consuming about six billion output tokens.

Why it matters — It's the last of Freek Wiedijk's famous 100 formalization challenges to fall, arriving far sooner than mathematicians expected — though Anthropic's own repository credits substantial prior work from Imperial College London's FLT project and Mathlib, tempering the fully-autonomous framing.

GPT-6 Astra Tops Terminal-Bench 4.0 via Codex at Half the Cost of the Runner-Up

OpenAI's Codex lead said GPT-6 Astra, launched September 3, finished #1 on the Terminal-Bench 4.0 agentic benchmark when run through the Codex harness, at roughly half the inference cost of the second-place model, as Astra's rollout to all ChatGPT Plus, Pro, and Business users wrapped up ahead of OpenAI's own multi-day estimate.

Why it matters — A concrete coding-benchmark result for Codex specifically, distinct from Astra's general-purpose launch.

"GitSpawn" Git-Config Flaw Gets Formal CVEs as Patch Gaps Persist

A week after Manifold Security disclosed GitSpawn — malicious repository git configs that run attacker commands in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and other coding agents before any trust prompt — GitHub formally assigned CVE-2026-72718 to the flaw in Goose (now patched) and CVE-2026-71963 to Hermes Agent, whose vendor never responded to disclosure attempts; a second attack path in Claude Code, reached via the `claude ultrareview` command, was still unfixed as of Manifold's last retest.

Why it matters — Shows which of the seven affected agents are actually fixed a week on — and that Claude Code still has an open path.

Google DeepMind Ships WeatherNext 3, Cutting Forecast Grids From 25km to 5km

Google DeepMind and Google Research introduced WeatherNext 3, a weather model that ingests real-time satellite data to issue a new global forecast every hour instead of every six, with roughly 5x sharper spatial resolution and up to 50% lower precipitation-forecast error than WeatherNext 2; it now powers forecasts in Search, the Gemini app, and Google Maps.

Why it matters — A concrete, independently-evaluated capability jump that ships straight into products billions of people already use.

Dev Tools & Releases

GitHub CLI's Linux Package Signing Key Expired September 5

The PGP key GitHub has used to sign `gh`'s APT and RPM Linux repositories expired Saturday; systems that picked up the updated keyring GitHub published back in April are unaffected, but anyone who set up the repository earlier, or who uses a custom image or automation path, needs to manually trust the replacement key or package updates will start failing signature verification.

Why it matters — A quiet key-rotation deadline that can silently break CI images and `apt upgrade`/`dnf update` on dev machines that missed it.

Why it matters — Small, concrete fixes for anyone testing multiple Astro builds side by side.

Bitcoin ETF Inflows Cool to $175M Friday After Record $731M Thursday Haul

U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs pulled in $174.6 million on Friday, a sharp slowdown from Thursday's $730.9 million — the largest single-day inflow since mid-January — as BTC's price settled near $79,600-$80,000, still off its recent high above $82,000 after the hot August jobs report revived Fed rate-hike expectations.

Why it matters — Demand hasn't dried up, but the pace of buying is normalizing after Thursday's spike.

On-Chain Data Shows Holders Cashed Out 110,000 BTC in Profits After the August Rally

CryptoQuant on-chain data shows Bitcoin holders realized profits on roughly 110,000 BTC in the weeks following August's rally from under $65,000 to near $80,000, including 23,000 BTC in net realized profit on August 21 alone — the heaviest single-day realization of 2026.

Why it matters — Concentrated profit-taking after a sharp rally has historically preceded corrections, though CryptoQuant's own Bull Score still reads above the threshold associated with sustainable bull markets.
