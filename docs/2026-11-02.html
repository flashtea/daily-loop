The UN mission in Western Sahara ended after 35 years when Russia vetoed its renewal and the Security Council adopted nothing in its place

A US draft extending MINURSO failed on 30 October by 11 votes to 1, Russia's veto, with China, Pakistan and Somalia abstaining; a Russian text extending the mandate to October 2027 drew three votes. The mandate expired at midnight on 31 October and the UN began liquidating the mission on 1 November, with Secretary-General Guterres urging Morocco and the Polisario Front to show maximum restraint.

why it mattersThe 1991 ceasefire between Morocco and the Polisario Front now has no UN observers behind it, and the Council has shown it cannot renew even a 35-year-old mission when Washington and Moscow disagree.

Iliana Yotova won Bulgaria's presidential runoff with about 58% of the vote on parallel counts, and Andrey Gyurov accepted the result

Polling agencies' full parallel counts put the acting president at 57.4–58.2% against 38.6–39.6% for the pro-Western former interim prime minister, on turnout of about 29%; official results follow on Monday. Yotova, who has been acting president since Rumen Radev resigned in January, favours dialogue with Moscow and opposes military aid to Ukraine.

why it mattersA NATO and euro-area member on the Black Sea now has an elected head of state who opposes arming Ukraine, so expect Bulgaria to be a brake rather than a driver on EU support for Kyiv.

OpenAI told subscribers that ChatGPT and Codex will draw from one shared usage allowance, with Unlimited Instant chat excluded

An updated help article and emails to paying users say that in the coming weeks use of ChatGPT and Codex on plans including Plus and Pro will count against a single allowance, visible with its reset time under Settings → Usage, while Instant-model chat stays outside it. OpenAI has not published the size of the allowance for any plan.

why it mattersIf you run Codex on a ChatGPT subscription, heavy agent sessions will now eat your chat budget too, so check the combined meter before leaning on both or move agent work to the API.

Binance reopened withdrawals for Bitcoin and 15 other networks two days after the $6.3 billion theft, saying it had replaced 95% of the stolen assets

The first phase ran from 06:00 UTC on 1 November and covered bitcoin, ether, USDT, USDC, BNB and XRP among others; Binance said further networks follow once each passes security and stability checks and gave no timetable. The FBI's alert listed 608 Ethereum, 19 Bitcoin and one Solana address for node operators and exchanges to block, and an on-chain analyst counted about $242 million laundered off Tron in two days.

why it mattersFunds on Binance are moving again, but anything you hold there on a network outside the first phase is still locked with no date, and the laundering is outrunning the freezes.
