Dev Tools & Releases

npm worm hijacks keyv and 400+ packages, plants Claude Code and VS Code hooks

Attackers compromised the maintainer behind keyv (127M weekly downloads) and its sibling caching packages, pushing a preinstall script that harvests GitHub, npm, cloud, and private-key credentials across a family with billions of monthly installs. The poisoned releases carried valid GitHub OIDC and SLSA provenance, and the keyv repo itself contained Claude Code and VS Code hooks that could trigger the payload when a workspace was opened.

Why it matters — Valid provenance signing meant standard supply-chain defenses didn't catch it, and the AI-tool hooks show attackers are now specifically targeting coding-agent workflows.

GitHub Spark stops accepting new users and new apps as of August 4, with existing users able to export their apps through August 31. GitHub says builders have shifted to Copilot's agentic workflows in VS Code, the CLI, and the Copilot app instead.

Why it matters — Follows GitHub Models' full retirement on July 30, consolidating GitHub's AI app-building story onto Copilot rather than standalone tools.

Bitcoin

Coldcard firmware bug has now drained $116M in a fourth wave of thefts

A 2021 firmware error routed seed generation on some Coldcard hardware wallets to a weak software PRNG instead of the hardware RNG, cutting effective entropy as low as 40 bits. Attackers have swept 1,800+ BTC from over 5,200 exposed addresses since July 30; Coinkite's CEO has urged all affected users to generate new seeds and move funds immediately.

Why it matters — A hardware-wallet flaw hitting cold storage undercuts the core pitch of self-custody, and researchers say a fourth sweep is still in progress.

Bitcoin.com News

Strategy sold $105M in Bitcoin below its average cost basis

Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) sold 1,638 BTC between July 27 and August 2 at an average price of $63,957, below its $75,419 acquisition cost, to fund dividends and buybacks on its STRC preferred stock. It's the company's third sale of 2026.

Why it matters — The largest corporate Bitcoin holder selling at a loss to cover preferred-stock obligations is a notable crack in the buy-and-hold treasury playbook.

CoinDesk

SEC freezes Nasdaq's Bitcoin index options after CME jurisdiction challenge

The SEC has stayed its own approval of Nasdaq PHLX's cash-settled Bitcoin index options (QBTC) after CME Group argued Bitcoin derivatives belong under CFTC jurisdiction, not the SEC's. Public comments are due August 24, with no timetable set for a final decision.

Why it matters — A jurisdictional turf fight between regulators could set precedent for who oversees Bitcoin derivatives products going forward.
