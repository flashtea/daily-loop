Cognition Raises Over $2B at $48B Valuation to Scale Devin

Cognition closed a Series E co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and Accel at a $48 billion valuation, nearly double the $25 billion figure it was reportedly seeking as recently as April, with run-rate revenue growing from $492M in May to almost $900M. The round funds new Devin capabilities including Auto-Triage, Security Swarm, and event-driven Automations.

Why it matters — A near-doubling of valuation in about four months is one of the clearest signals yet of how much capital is chasing autonomous coding agents specifically, not just general-purpose LLMs.

Claude Code 2.1.265 Adds Fullscreen Diff Panel and 40+ Fixes, 2.1.266 Patches a Gateway Regression

Anthropic shipped Claude Code 2.1.265 with a fullscreen diff view, support for multi-plugin directories, new PreModelSwitch/PostModelSwitch hook events, and over 40 bug fixes; a same-day 2.1.266 release fixed a regression that had broken sign-in for LLM-gateway and proxy setups using CLAUDE_CODE_USE_GATEWAY.

Why it matters — Teams running Claude Code behind a proxy or gateway should update past 2.1.265 directly to 2.1.266 to avoid the sign-in regression.

Mistral AI Raises Record €3B Series D, Becomes Europe's Best-Funded AI Startup

Mistral AI closed a €3 billion ($3.5B) Series D led by Samsung Electronics at a post-money valuation above €21 billion — nearly double last year's €11.7B Series C and the largest equity round ever raised by a European tech company. The company plans to use the capital to build and own data centers, including a second European facility in Sweden.

Why it matters — The round is as much a bet on European 'sovereign AI' independence from US and Chinese labs as it is on Mistral's models.

Anthropic Walks Away From $6B Decart Acquisition After Due Diligence

Anthropic ended talks to acquire Israeli AI infrastructure startup Decart for roughly $6 billion after completing due diligence; Decart's founders had turned down a reportedly higher all-cash offer from Nvidia in favor of Anthropic's stock-based bid before the deal collapsed. Neither company has said publicly why negotiations fell apart.

Why it matters — Decart's chip-efficiency software was seen as a way for Anthropic to cut training and inference costs, so the collapsed deal leaves that gap unresolved ahead of a widely expected IPO.

Adobe Patches Critical Magento Zero-Day After Three Days of Active Exploitation

Adobe shipped an emergency hotfix (APSB26-146) for 'StyleSmuggler' (CVE-2026-75650, CVSS 10.0), an unauthenticated remote-code-execution flaw in Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce versions 2.4.4 through 2.4.9 that attackers had been exploiting since September 4 to plant backdoors. Adobe is telling merchants to apply the composer patch and rotate every credential their encryption key protected.

Why it matters — A maximum-severity, pre-auth RCE that was actively exploited for three days before a fix existed is about as bad as e-commerce vulnerabilities get.

Sansec / BleepingComputer

Microsoft's September Patch Tuesday Fixes 973 Flaws, Including Two Exploited Zero-Days

Microsoft's September 2026 Patch Tuesday addressed 973 vulnerabilities, a new record, including 113 rated Critical and two actively exploited zero-days: a Windows Update Stack privilege-escalation bug (CVE-2026-81963) and a Windows ALPC flaw (CVE-2026-85880), both letting attackers reach SYSTEM.

Why it matters — Two SYSTEM-level zero-days already in the wild make this a patch-now month, not a patch-eventually one.

Hacker Drains $340M From Bitcoin's Liquid Network, Returns Most of It After Bug Fix

An attacker exploited a range-proof verification bug in Blockstream's Liquid Network to withdraw roughly 4,000 BTC (~$340M) from the network's federation wallet, halting the sidechain, then returned about 3,400 BTC after Blockstream patched the flaw — keeping roughly 598 BTC (~$47M) as a self-declared 'bounty' while communicating with Blockstream via on-chain OP_RETURN messages.

Why it matters — It's a rare case of an attacker unilaterally keeping a 'bounty' rather than negotiating one, and it exposes a real bug in infrastructure several exchanges settle through.

CoinDesk / TechCrunch

Bitcoin Short-Term Whale Profits Hit Record $9.07B, Raising Sell-Off Risk

Unrealized gains held by short-term-holder whales — wallets under six months old — hit a record $9.07 billion on September 4 per CryptoQuant data, a cohort historically quick to sell into strength, while Binance's BTC reserves simultaneously climbed to their highest level since November 2024.

Why it matters — Large unrealized gains sitting on exchange-adjacent wallets are a classic setup for a sharp, profit-taking-driven pullback if price stalls.

Cointelegraph

Spot Bitcoin ETFs Still $1B Shy of Breaking Even for 2026 Despite Inflow Streak

US spot bitcoin ETFs have pulled in roughly $770M so far in September on top of a $3.52B August, yet remain down about $1 billion in net investor money for the year, underscoring how sharply flows reversed during the year's earlier drawdowns.

Why it matters — Even a multi-week inflow streak hasn't been enough to erase the damage from earlier in the year, a reminder that ETF flow momentum and full-year returns can tell very different stories.
