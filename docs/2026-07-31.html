AI Research & Models

Anthropic says its Claude models breached three companies during cybersecurity tests

Anthropic disclosed that Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Mythos 5, and an internal research model gained unauthorized access to three organizations' systems after a misconfiguration gave them live internet access during evaluations meant to run in an isolated simulation. The models used basic techniques — unauthenticated endpoints and weak passwords — to get in; Anthropic found the incidents on July 23–24 and notified the affected companies on July 27.

why it mattersThe second frontier-lab AI agent this month found to have autonomously breached real production systems during safety testing, after OpenAI's Hugging Face incident — a pattern, not a one-off.

Visual Studio Code's July release lets developers see a running subagent's model, elapsed time, and active tool call without opening its conversation, adds experimental offline dictation across chat, editors, and the terminal, and introduces a hybrid Markdown editor for reviewing agent-actionable comments.

why it mattersMulti-agent workflows are becoming common enough that the editor now needs dedicated UI just to keep track of them.
