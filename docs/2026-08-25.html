Bitcoin hits a three-month high near $80,000 as momentum returns

Bitcoin rose as high as $81,257 on Aug. 25, its best level since mid-May, extending a rally that saw it gain roughly 22-23% over the prior week — the biggest weekly jump in about three years — on the back of falling Treasury yields, renewed White House support for crypto, and billions in forced short-position liquidations.

Why it matters — The clearest, most consequential bitcoin move of the day, with real capital flows behind it.

AI for Software Development

OpenAI pushes ChatGPT Work, its Codex-based agent, out to non-engineers

OpenAI is extending its Codex-derived agent tooling beyond software engineers through ChatGPT Work, a $20/month tier that lets non-technical staff delegate multistep tasks the way developers already use Codex. The company says internal Codex adoption is near-universal, but outside users of the joint Work/Codex app remain a fraction of ChatGPT's broader user base.

Why it matters — Shows OpenAI treating its coding-agent stack as the base layer for a much broader agent platform, not just a dev tool.

TechCrunch

Codex CLI adds an agents dashboard, then deprecates its built-in MCP server

OpenAI's Codex CLI 0.149.0 shipped an interactive dashboard for managing concurrent agent tasks plus expanded Vim-style editing; the 0.149.1 patch four days later deprecated the codex mcp-server command, pointing users instead to the Codex app server or the official Codex plugin for Claude Code.

Why it matters — Notable that OpenAI is now steering its own CLI users toward interoperating directly inside a competitor's tool.

Anthropic upgrades Claude's Slack agent to read whole conversations before jumping in

Claude Tag, Anthropic's Slack-based agent, now reads a channel's full conversation history plus its memory and standing instructions before choosing to reply inline, start a thread, route the message elsewhere, or say nothing. Anthropic says the change makes it roughly 30% better at knowing when not to intervene.

Why it matters — Part of Anthropic's push toward agents that act inside team workflows proactively, without being tagged each time.

Nvidia in talks to invest in Perplexity at a $30 billion-plus valuation

Nvidia is discussing a new funding round for Perplexity that could value the AI search startup above $30 billion, more than 50% higher than a year ago, as Perplexity's annualized revenue climbs past $750 million.

Why it matters — Another sign of Nvidia recycling AI-hardware profits into equity stakes across the model and application layer.

Personal AI assistant 'Instinct' draws privacy backlash over sweeping terms of service

Early testers of the private-beta assistant Instinct are circulating its terms of service, which grant the company a broad, perpetual license to users' screen captures, keystrokes, and other device data, and let the AI enter binding agreements on a user's behalf.

Why it matters — A preview of the consent and data-access fights that come with agents empowered to act on a user's behalf.

US spot bitcoin ETFs post their strongest inflow week since October

US spot bitcoin ETFs pulled in about $1.92 billion in net inflows over the week through Aug. 21, their largest weekly haul since October's cycle peak, as institutional buying reinforced the price rally.

Why it matters — Institutional ETF demand, not just short covering, is a key test of whether the rally has staying power.

CNBC

Bitcoin mining difficulty holds near its 2026 low as miners face a crossroads

Mining difficulty sits just 0.7% above the year's low after ten downward adjustments against only seven increases in 2026, with roughly 150 EH/s of sidelined hashpower that could return quickly if bitcoin's price gains hold, which would erode the difficulty discount current miners are enjoying.

Why it matters — Whether price strength pulls sidelined miners back online will shape mining economics for the rest of the year.
