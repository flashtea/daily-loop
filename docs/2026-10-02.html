OpenClaw Foundation launches free enterprise control plane for persistent AI agents

OpenClaw Enterprise is an MIT-licensed control plane adding multi-tenancy, security boundaries, role-based access and audit trails for always-on agents. The project began inside OpenAI, moved to the independent OpenClaw Foundation, and was developed with Red Hat and NVIDIA. It targets internal pilots, with a 1.0 release planned later in 2026.

why it mattersGovernance and isolation for coding agents are the main blockers to running them in production.
