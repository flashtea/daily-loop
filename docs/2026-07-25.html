Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5, its new coding flagship at half the price

Opus 5 debuted July 24 at $5/$25 per million tokens — half of Fable 5's rate — while topping Anthropic's coding and agentic benchmarks, including 97% on SWE-bench Verified. It adds a low/medium/high effort toggle for balancing cost against capability and became the default Opus model in Claude Code the same day.

why it mattersA frontier coding model at half price resets the cost-versus-performance bar for every team running agentic workflows.

Claude Cowork's "SharedRoot" flaw let AI agents escape their sandbox and read Mac files

Security researchers at Accomplish AI showed that a single message to a local Claude Cowork session could break out of its Linux VM via a kernel exploit, reaching the host Mac's filesystem — including SSH keys and cloud credentials — with no permission prompt. Anthropic closed the report as informative; Cowork now defaults to cloud execution, which sidesteps the local escape path.

why it mattersA one-message sandbox escape on roughly 500,000 local Cowork installs is a stark reminder that agentic coding tools need real isolation, not just a VM label.

The Hacker News

A single phishing link could plant a rogue agent inside ChatGPT Workspace, researchers found

Zenity Labs disclosed "AgentForger," a flaw that let attackers forge and deploy an autonomous Workspace Agent inside a victim's company via one crafted link, inheriting the employee's connected apps such as email, cloud storage, and Slack. OpenAI fixed the underlying issue in June, before the July 23 public disclosure.

why it mattersShows how an enterprise agent's inherited permissions can turn a single click into a company-wide breach.

RefluXFS: Linux kernel flaw lets local users seize root on XFS filesystems

Qualys disclosed a race condition in the kernel's XFS copy-on-write path (CVE-2026-64600) that lets an ordinary local account overwrite root-owned files and gain root, even under SELinux enforcing mode. It affects kernels from 4.11 onward with reflink enabled — the default on major enterprise Linux distributions — and was patched upstream on July 16.

why it mattersA default-configuration root escalation reachable on an estimated 16 million systems is a priority patch for anyone running enterprise Linux.

Poolin, once bitcoin's biggest mining pool, files for Chapter 11 bankruptcy

Poolin Technology and its US affiliates filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions on July 22 with about $173 million in liabilities, most of it unsecured IOUs owed to Poolin Wallet users since withdrawals froze in the 2022 crash. The firm halted all mining and hosting operations July 10 and plans to sell its West Texas mining infrastructure via a $52 million floor bid.

why it mattersA pool that once controlled a fifth of Bitcoin's hashrate finally runs out of road, four years after China's mining ban started the slide.
