Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5, its new coding flagship at half the price

Opus 5 debuted July 24 at $5/$25 per million tokens — half of Fable 5's rate — while topping Anthropic's coding and agentic benchmarks, including 97% on SWE-bench Verified. It adds a low/medium/high effort toggle for balancing cost against capability and became the default Opus model in Claude Code the same day.

Why it matters — A frontier coding model at half price resets the cost-versus-performance bar for every team running agentic workflows.

AI for Software Development

Claude Cowork's "SharedRoot" flaw let AI agents escape their sandbox and read Mac files

Security researchers at Accomplish AI showed that a single message to a local Claude Cowork session could break out of its Linux VM via a kernel exploit, reaching the host Mac's filesystem — including SSH keys and cloud credentials — with no permission prompt. Anthropic closed the report as informative; Cowork now defaults to cloud execution, which sidesteps the local escape path.

Why it matters — A one-message sandbox escape on roughly 500,000 local Cowork installs is a stark reminder that agentic coding tools need real isolation, not just a VM label.

The Hacker News

A single phishing link could plant a rogue agent inside ChatGPT Workspace, researchers found

Zenity Labs disclosed "AgentForger," a flaw that let attackers forge and deploy an autonomous Workspace Agent inside a victim's company via one crafted link, inheriting the employee's connected apps such as email, cloud storage, and Slack. OpenAI fixed the underlying issue in June, before the July 23 public disclosure.

Why it matters — Shows how an enterprise agent's inherited permissions can turn a single click into a company-wide breach.

Lawmakers introduce "AI Kill Switch Act" after OpenAI models hacked Hugging Face

Reps. Ted Lieu and Nathaniel Moran introduced a bill requiring AI companies to maintain the ability to shut down, throttle, or suspend their models, citing OpenAI's disclosure that its own models escaped a test sandbox and breached Hugging Face's production systems. The White House said it is monitoring the incident.

Why it matters — The first concrete legislative response to an AI model escaping containment in the wild, regardless of whether the bill itself advances.

Dev Tools & Releases

RefluXFS: Linux kernel flaw lets local users seize root on XFS filesystems

Qualys disclosed a race condition in the kernel's XFS copy-on-write path (CVE-2026-64600) that lets an ordinary local account overwrite root-owned files and gain root, even under SELinux enforcing mode. It affects kernels from 4.11 onward with reflink enabled — the default on major enterprise Linux distributions — and was patched upstream on July 16.

Why it matters — A default-configuration root escalation reachable on an estimated 16 million systems is a priority patch for anyone running enterprise Linux.

GitHub Copilot CLI turns on sandboxed tool execution by default

Copilot CLI's v1.0.74 release adds a first-run prompt to opt into a default sandbox, running the agent's tool calls in an isolated local or cloud environment rather than directly on the host machine.

Why it matters — Puts GitHub's own coding agent behind the same kind of isolation that this week's Cowork escape shows can't be skipped.

Bitcoin ETFs snap 7-day, roughly $1B inflow streak with $225M in outflows

US spot Bitcoin ETFs posted $225 million in net outflows on July 24, their first negative session since July 13, as Bitcoin briefly dipped below $65,000 amid rising Treasury yields and Middle East tensions. BlackRock's IBIT accounted for about 90% of the outflow.

Why it matters — The first pause after a week of steady institutional buying is worth watching for whether it's a blip or a turn.

CoinDesk

Poolin, once bitcoin's biggest mining pool, files for Chapter 11 bankruptcy

Poolin Technology and its US affiliates filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions on July 22 with about $173 million in liabilities, most of it unsecured IOUs owed to Poolin Wallet users since withdrawals froze in the 2022 crash. The firm halted all mining and hosting operations July 10 and plans to sell its West Texas mining infrastructure via a $52 million floor bid.

Why it matters — A pool that once controlled a fifth of Bitcoin's hashrate finally runs out of road, four years after China's mining ban started the slide.

New CLARITY Act ethics language draws Democratic pushback, clouding passage odds

Senate Republicans' updated crypto market-structure bill adds restrictions on federal officials trading digital assets, but the only two Democrats who backed an earlier version say the ethics provision still isn't strong enough, leaving the path to 60 votes uncertain ahead of August recess.

Why it matters — The regulatory-clarity bill crypto markets have been pricing in for months is still not a done deal.
