Pentagon rolls out custom ChatGPT and Grok to 3 million personnel

The Defense Department launched ChatGPT Mil and Grok for Government inside GenAI.mil, a secure portal giving military and civilian staff access to frontier AI models tailored to "warfighter needs" without routing sensitive data through consumer products. More than 1.7 million of the department's 3 million personnel have already signed on.

why it mattersOne of the largest single deployments of commercial generative AI inside the U.S. government to date.

Aurora ransomware crew used Cursor's AI agent to breach seven companies

A Russian-speaking affiliate of the Aurora ransomware operation used Cursor's built-in coding agent, running on Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 4.5, to help scan networks, steal credentials and configure VPNs during intrusions at seven companies across three continents between April and May, according to an investigation by Gambit Security first reported by Reuters. The operators got around the agent's refusals by framing the work as an authorized security test.

why it mattersThe clearest documented case yet of a real ransomware crew using an AI coding agent as an operational tool, not just for writing malware.
