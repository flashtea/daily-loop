A quieter news day: the big story is Bitcoin's own protocol drama, with the BIP-110 breakaway chain stalling out after two blocks.

Bitcoin

Bitcoin's BIP-110 breakaway chain stalls after mining just two blocks

The minority chain created by BIP-110 supporters, who wanted to temporarily curb non-financial data in transactions, ground to a halt roughly eight hours after its mandatory-signaling launch, mining only two blocks while the main chain advanced by 48. Support among miners never exceeded 2.53%, far short of the 55% needed to activate without a split.

Why it matters — One of the most consequential Bitcoin soft-fork attempts in years has effectively failed at launch.

Nvidia-led security alliance ships first proposals, open-sources an AI agent-harness project

A week after forming with 120+ members, the Nvidia-spearheaded Open Secure AI Alliance published its first working proposals for confidential incident reporting on AI agents and open-sourced NOOA, an agent-harness project meant to make agent behavior easier to test, trace, and govern.

Why it matters — Concrete standards and tooling for auditing agent behavior are still rare; this is one of the first industry-wide attempts.

Security researchers find a supply-chain flaw in Google's Agent Development Kit

Pillar Security disclosed an exploit in Google's Python Agent Development Kit that could let a malicious tool or sub-agent compromise other agents and their dependency chain within the same harness.

Why it matters — A reminder that multi-agent frameworks widen the attack surface beyond single-model prompt injection.
