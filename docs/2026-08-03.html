Unlike after FTX, the Coldcard exploit is sending bitcoin back to exchanges

With losses from the Coldcard firmware flaw near $89 million across three attack waves and 4,585 addresses, holders are reversing the post-FTX playbook: daily exchange deposits under 10 BTC jumped to 7,300 BTC on July 31, the highest since February, as spooked self-custody users move funds to platforms they consider safer for now.

why it mattersA hardware-wallet failure is doing what an exchange collapse usually does, but in reverse — pushing coins toward custodians instead of away from them.

Node.js patches HTTP/2, DNS, TLS, and permission-model vulnerabilities

Node.js's July security release fixes a memory-exhaustion flaw in HTTP/2 header handling, a heap-use-after-free from re-entrant HTTP/2 calls, a denial-of-service in dns.resolveAny(), an incomplete TLS hostname-verification fix, and over-permissive filesystem access in the Permission Model. Updates cover the 22.x, 24.x, and 26.x release lines.

why it mattersSeveral of these are remotely triggerable; teams on affected lines should update promptly.
