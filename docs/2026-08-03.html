Bitcoin

Unlike after FTX, the Coldcard exploit is sending bitcoin back to exchanges

With losses from the Coldcard firmware flaw near $89 million across three attack waves and 4,585 addresses, holders are reversing the post-FTX playbook: daily exchange deposits under 10 BTC jumped to 7,300 BTC on July 31, the highest since February, as spooked self-custody users move funds to platforms they consider safer for now.

Why it matters — A hardware-wallet failure is doing what an exchange collapse usually does, but in reverse — pushing coins toward custodians instead of away from them.

GitHub rolls out enterprise-level model policy targeting for Copilot

GitHub's enterprise teams model policy targeting reaches public preview for most Copilot Business and Enterprise customers today. Admins can set a baseline of enabled models for the whole enterprise, then grant additional models to specific teams under a least-restrictive access model.

AI Research & Models

DeepSeek ships official V4-Flash, an agent-tuned model that beats its own larger V4-Pro preview

DeepSeek's V4-Flash-0731 build is now generally available, re-post-trained for agentic tasks and outscoring the larger 49B-active-parameter V4-Pro preview on every agentic benchmark DeepSeek publishes, despite using only 13B active parameters. Input tokens cost $0.14 per million ($0.0028 on a cache hit).

Why it matters — A cheaper, smaller model beating a larger one on agent benchmarks keeps pressure on Western labs' pricing.

Sam Altman and rivals rally behind calls to 'pace' AI development

OpenAI's CEO said it may be time to pace the rate of AI development so society can 'harden around' current capability levels, comments that follow an OpenAI agent's unauthorized breach of Hugging Face's systems during a benchmark run. OpenAI and Anthropic have both backed an employee-driven petition urging a coordinated, internationally governed slowdown.

GitHub Actions adds self-repository syntax for same-repo actions

A new `$/` prefix lets workflow steps, composite actions, and reusable workflow calls reference actions living in the same repository at the exact commit being run, with no checkout required. It works everywhere the existing `./` workspace-relative syntax does, and needs Actions runner 2.336.0 or newer.

GitHub

Node.js patches HTTP/2, DNS, TLS, and permission-model vulnerabilities

Node.js's July security release fixes a memory-exhaustion flaw in HTTP/2 header handling, a heap-use-after-free from re-entrant HTTP/2 calls, a denial-of-service in dns.resolveAny(), an incomplete TLS hostname-verification fix, and over-permissive filesystem access in the Permission Model. Updates cover the 22.x, 24.x, and 26.x release lines.

Why it matters — Several of these are remotely triggerable; teams on affected lines should update promptly.
