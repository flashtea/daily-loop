AI for Software Development

OpenAI moves to cut Cursor off its models after SpaceX acquisition

OpenAI notified SpaceX it will wind down the contract giving Cursor access to OpenAI models, with a proposed shutoff date of November 12, saying it cannot trust SpaceX to honor its terms of service given Elon Musk's history of contract disputes. Anthropic co-founder Tom Brown said Anthropic will keep increasing Claude compute for Cursor instead.

Why it matters — OpenAI models made up only about 5% of Cursor's traffic, but the move turns model access into a competitive weapon right as SpaceX's AI arm and Anthropic jockey for position in coding tools.

Claude Code patches symlink bugs that could bypass permission checks

Anthropic shipped Claude Code 2.1.251, fixing bugs where file tools, plugin commands, Grep/Glob searches and the Workflow tool could follow symlinks to read or write outside approved locations after permission checks had already passed.

Why it matters — A concrete fix for the class of sandbox-escape bug that matters most as agentic coding tools get broader file-system access.

AI Research & Models

Google moves its Gemini AI safety team into its lobbying division

Google is relocating its roughly 90-person AI responsibility unit, which evaluates Gemini for chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear risks, from DeepMind to report to Kent Walker's global-affairs organization starting September 1. Several team members who sought transfers to stay inside DeepMind had those requests denied.

Why it matters — Placing the team that flags dangerous-capability findings inside the division that manages Google's relationships with regulators raises an obvious independence question.

Quartz

Z.ai releases GLM-5.3-Flash, a 320B open-weight model under MIT license

Z.ai launched GLM-5.3-Flash, a natively multimodal 320-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 18 billion active parameters and a 1-million-token context window, publishing the weights on Hugging Face under the MIT license at roughly a tenth of its predecessor's price.

Why it matters — Day-one open weights scoring close to Claude Opus 4.8 on coding benchmarks keep pushing the price of capable models toward zero.

Z.ai

OpenAI's first custom inference chip beats Nvidia's Blackwell in published benchmarks

OpenAI and Broadcom showed the first benchmarks for Jalapeno, OpenAI's in-house inference chip, claiming 1.5x-1.9x more AI work per watt and up to 3.6x lower latency than Nvidia's Blackwell systems while drawing 700W versus 1,200-1,400W, with results partly verified on-site by SemiAnalysis on its public InferenceX benchmark.

Why it matters — The first credible outside evidence that a hyperscaler's custom silicon can beat Nvidia on inference efficiency, not just cost.

Dev Tools & Releases

VS Code 1.135 lets you continue Copilot or Claude agent sessions started elsewhere

The release adds external agent sessions so developers can pick up Copilot or Claude Code runs started in other apps, an experimental 'Rubber Duck' feature that gets a second model's opinion on your work, and a streamlined agents window with per-model token usage breakdowns.

Why it matters — Microsoft is building VS Code into a control surface for multiple competing coding agents rather than betting on just one.

Amazon to shut down Mechanical Turk and SageMaker Ground Truth on September 30

Amazon confirmed it will close Mechanical Turk, the crowdsourced task marketplace it launched in 2005, along with SageMaker Ground Truth and Augmented AI, on September 30, ending Amazon's human-data-labeling infrastructure as newer AI-training-data startups have taken over the market.

Why it matters — The platform Bezos once called 'artificial artificial intelligence' outlasted the joke by two decades before AI itself made it redundant.

Bitcoin

Bitcoin extends slide to $77,678 as Fed rate-hike odds climb to 68%

Bitcoin fell a further 3.3% to $77,678 on August 29, continuing Friday's drop from above $80,000, as markets kept repricing Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole remarks, pushing the odds of a September rate hike to 68% from roughly 40% before the speech.

Why it matters — A second straight day of Fed-driven selling shows the rate-cut hopes behind bitcoin's summer rally are still unwinding.

CoinDesk

Spot Bitcoin ETFs post $202M outflow, ending nine-day inflow streak

U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs saw $201.8 million in net outflows on Friday, breaking a nine-session run that had pulled in roughly $3 billion since August 17, with ARKB leading redemptions at $114.9 million while BlackRock's IBIT saw a comparatively modest $33.4 million withdrawn.

Why it matters — The first outflow day in two and a half weeks tests whether institutional demand can keep absorbing bitcoin's pullback.
