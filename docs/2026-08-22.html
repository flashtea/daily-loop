Nvidia research: the agent harness matters more than the model for long-horizon tasks

Nvidia found that a custom harness — tuned memory management plus a supervisor component — took Claude Opus 5 from a 30% score (the best of any model tested) to 100% on the ARC-AGI-3 reasoning benchmark. Databricks has separately found harness choice can double or halve agentic costs.

why it mattersReframes where the competitive edge in agentic coding tools actually lives — orchestration and tooling, not just the underlying LLM.

The Register

Backdoored versions of popular Rust crate arrayref hit crates.io

A malicious release of arrayref — a crate with roughly 245 million lifetime downloads — was published to crates.io carrying a hidden dependency on a typosquatted package that executed build-time malware. It was pulled within about 86 minutes, but the incident adds to a run of registry supply-chain attacks this year.

why it mattersA high-download crate being compromised, even briefly, is a reminder that package-registry trust remains a weak link.
