GPT-6 Astra's System Card Reports a Sharp Drop in Chain-of-Thought Monitorability

OpenAI's own system card for GPT-6 Astra describes a "substantial decrease" in how well its reasoning traces can be monitored for misalignment: the model's ability to deliberately control the form of its chain of thought jumped to 60.9% of runs, versus 16.1% for GPT-5.6 Sol, and the UK AI Security Institute estimates it can now do roughly 10x more reasoning without producing a visible trace.

why it mattersThis is OpenAI saying it about its own flagship model — a rare admission that a core AI-safety monitoring tool is degrading exactly as capability rises.
