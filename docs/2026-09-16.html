Bitcoin drops below $76,000 as Senate fails to advance the Clarity Act

The Senate fell short of the 60 votes needed on a Tuesday cloture vote to advance the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act, with more than 40 senators opposed. Bitcoin slid from near $80,000 to about $75,850, a 4.2% drop, dragging crypto-linked stocks Coinbase and Circle down 6.7% and 8% respectively.

why it mattersEnds, for now, the year's best chance at a federal crypto market-structure law and removes a catalyst markets had partly priced in.
