AI Research & Models

OpenAI said its models disproved the Exponential Time Hypothesis, published the Lean proof, and handed verification to Impagliazzo, Paturi and Williams

OpenAI announced three AI-produced results on Monday: a subexponential-time, polynomial-space algorithm for counting k-SAT solutions that refutes the Exponential Time Hypothesis (natural-language disproof on 16 October, Lean formalization the next day, now in its public math repository), a proof of the local smoothing conjecture that it says resolves the Kakeya and Stein restriction conjectures in all dimensions, and an existence proof for four-dimensional Yang–Mills and QCD that a group led by David Gross is writing up before release. Russell Impagliazzo and Ramamohan Paturi, who proposed the hypothesis in 1999, and Ryan Williams of MIT are checking the ETH result, Williams told TechCrunch that OpenAI said it had checked for cryptographic breaks and found none, and Anthropic published its own ETH refutation on GitHub hours later after its mathematician Levent Alpöge said OpenAI's work built on existing research.

why it mattersAn assumption behind hundreds of complexity papers now rests on a machine-checked proof no human has fully read, so treat frontier models as producers of real mathematics and watch the reviewers' verdict for what it says about hardness assumptions in cryptography.

why it mattersWho governs Israel decides whether the Iran war resumes and whether Hormuz reopens, so wait for Friday's full count and the president's mandate rather than election-night claims.

AI Research & Models4 stories, lead above

Axiom Math published a complete Lean formalization of the classification of finite simple groups, 43.1 million lines written by AI agents in 24 days

The 15,000-page proof, spread over more than 500 articles by about 100 mathematicians, is now checked by Lean's kernel end to end; AxiomProver's agents, run on a changing mix of models under human direction, decomposed, searched, wrote and reviewed the proof, and found that a 1986 argument in the Unbalanced Group Theorem assumed an operation stayed inside a family of groups when it could leave it, which the team closed with a new argument. The repository and an errata list are public.

why it mattersFormal verification at the scale of an entire field is now a job for agent swarms, and the gaps they found in published mathematics are the case for running the same machinery over the proofs and protocols you depend on.

Axiom Math

Google DeepMind released Genie 4, a world model that generates explorable environments with native audio, as a research preview with Gemini API early access

Genie 4 generates interactive scenes in real time with ambient sound, music and dialogue, is steered in natural language with Gemini models keeping the world coherent, and watermarks all output with SynthID. It is open to trusted testers now, is coming to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and to early-access partners of the Gemini API and AI Studio, and DeepMind lists visual drift and loss of object permanence over long sessions as known limits.

why it mattersGenerated interactive environments are reaching the API, so if you build games, simulations or training data for agents and robots, plan on generating test worlds rather than authoring them.

Cloudflare disclosed that an SGLang bug let 155 Workers AI responses from GLM-5.3 contain other users' KV cache data

Between the model's launch on 27 August and 9 September, prompts longer than the 681,280-token prefill cache pool were rejected but not retired, so decode nodes attended over stale cache pages from earlier requests and returned them with HTTP 200; a Knostic researcher reported it through HackerOne, Cloudflare found no evidence of deliberate exploitation, the fixes are upstream in SGLang v0.5.22, and the disclosure was published on Monday.

why it mattersIf you serve models with SGLang in disaggregated prefill/decode mode, upgrade to v0.5.22 and check that the KV pool is at least as large as the context window.

Brazil's Ibovespa fell almost 14% and the real 8% the day after Lula's re-election, triggering the first circuit breaker since 2020

B3 halted trading for 30 minutes on Monday after the index fell 10% at the open; it touched a 17% loss intraday and closed down almost 14%, its worst day since March 2020, while the real fell as much as 12% against the dollar before closing 8% lower. The move reversed a rally that had put the index at a record after Flávio Bolsonaro's first-round lead, prediction markets had given him over 80% odds, and he has not conceded.

why it mattersA repricing this violent on an election the markets had already called is the argument for sizing any emerging-market position for a surprise rather than for the quoted odds.
