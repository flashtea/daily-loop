Money

Binance lost about $6.3 billion to an attacker who reached a service container through a DevOps engineer's laptop, the largest crypto theft on record

The withdrawals began at 20:05 UTC on 29 October and drained hot wallets on ten chains, including 18,270 BTC and about $3.8 billion of assets on Ethereum; Binance suspended deposits and withdrawals, said no private key leaked, pledged to cover all losses from its SAFU fund and company money, and reported about $3 billion frozen on-chain, with Tether and Circle alone freezing more than $2.5 billion of stablecoins. TRM Labs put the theft at $6.36 billion, four times the 2025 Bybit hack, and attributed it with high confidence to North Korea's TraderTraitor unit; bitcoin fell below $77,000 on the news.

why it mattersMove anything you hold on Binance to self-custody once withdrawals reopen, and treat any exchange balance as an unsecured claim on a company whose hot wallets were emptied in twenty minutes.

OpenAI's release notes for 29 October say Pro 100 and Pro 500 now carry twice their previous Work and Codex allowance, that the Pro 200 reduction announced by email on 29 September is cancelled, and that Pro 200 subscribers who had already been moved to the lower allowance are doubled back up to the same limit; prices are unchanged. why it mattersIf you planned to downgrade or leave Pro 200 before tomorrow's cut, you can stay, and a Pro 100 seat now buys twice the Codex work it did yesterday.

AI Research & Models1 story

Mistral published the weights of Mistral Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter model, under a modified MIT licence that excludes model-as-a-service providers

The checkpoint (mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B on Hugging Face, 52 billion active parameters) includes further reinforcement learning since the 6 October API preview; Mistral quoted 48% on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index, tied with GLM-5.3 while using 47% fewer tokens per task, and 41% on Terminal-Bench 4.0. The Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight licence allows commercial use and fine-tuning but requires businesses that run a model-as-a-service offering to buy a separate licence, a step back from the Apache 2.0 terms of Mistral Large 3; API pricing is $0.70 per million input tokens and $2.10 per million output.

why it mattersA GLM-5.3-class open coding model is now available from a European vendor, but read the licence before serving it to others, because the MaaS carve-out means it is not open source in the OSI sense.

Money4 stories, lead above

The Bank of Japan held at 1.25% on an 8–1 vote and, for the first time since adopting the 2% target in 2013, judged underlying inflation generally consistent with it

Hajime Takata dissented in favour of 1.5%, arguing the bank had entered a new phase and should move faster toward neutral; the board also raised its fiscal 2027 inflation forecast to 2.6% from 2.4% and voted unanimously to raise the pace of ETF sales from about 330 billion yen to about 860 billion yen a year at book value from January 2027. Markets priced about an 80% chance of a hike in December before the meeting.

why it mattersThe BoJ has declared its inflation job done, so plan for Japanese rates to keep rising into 2027 and for yen funding to keep getting dearer.

The ECB held its deposit rate at 2.5% and said Christine Lagarde will leave on 7 February 2027, eight months before her term ends

The Governing Council kept the deposit, refinancing and marginal lending rates at 2.50%, 2.65% and 2.90%, said rising energy prices tilt inflation risks up and growth risks down, and Lagarde said the council debated a hike before voting unanimously to hold. The ECB separately announced that she had told the council and European Council President António Costa she will step down early; the European Council picks a successor under Article 283, with Executive Board member Isabel Schnabel also leaving early in 2027 for the IMF.

why it mattersExpect a December hike debate to continue, and watch the November and December EU summits, because the ECB's president, an Executive Board member and its chief economist all change within six months.

European Central Bank

US GDP grew at a 3.4% annual rate in the third quarter while the PCE price index slowed to 2.1% from 5.0%

The BEA's advance estimate put growth up from 2.2% in the second quarter on investment, consumer and government spending, with core PCE at 2.5% against 3.3% before; the second estimate is due on 25 November. The print came a day after the Fed held at 3.75–4.00% with two dissents for a hike.

why it mattersGrowth above 3% with quarterly inflation back near target weakens the case for the December hike that two FOMC members voted for this week, so do not position for one yet.
