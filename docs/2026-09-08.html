OpenAI's Chief Scientist Says No Lab Has Solved Alignment, Calls for Voluntary Slowdowns

In an essay titled 'An Alien Mind,' OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki wrote that no AI lab, including his own, has solved alignment and monitoring well enough to keep scaling at maximum speed, singled out chain-of-thought monitoring as an eroding safety net, and called for voluntary industry slowdowns and mandatory outside-audited safety standards.

Why it matters — This is OpenAI's own chief scientist, not a critic, saying the industry's main tool for catching a misbehaving model before it acts is getting less reliable as capability rises.

OpenAI shipped Codex CLI 0.153.4, making GPT-6 Astra the default model in the bundled picker and refining its guidance on when to ask asynchronous clarifying questions, following a string of Astra-focused Codex point releases earlier in the week.

Bun 1.4.2 Fixes Elysia Build Regression and AsyncLocalStorage Leak

Bun 1.4.2 patches two regressions introduced in 1.4.1 — a bundler bug that broke Elysia apps and an AsyncLocalStorage memory leak — along with a worker_threads ordering bug, JIT and musl GC crashes, and CMYK/YCCK JPEG decoding in Bun.Image.

Why it matters — A fast follow-up for anyone who hit the 1.4.1 regressions after last month's Rust rewrite.

Starlette/LiteLLM Auth-Bypass Chain Still Under Active Ransomware Exploitation

CISA's addition of Starlette (CVE-2026-48710) and LiteLLM (CVE-2026-59822) to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog is still driving active attacks: Qilin ransomware operators are chaining the two flaws for unauthenticated remote code execution, deploying XMRig miners and harvesting API keys, with a federal patch deadline of September 16.

Why it matters — Any team running LiteLLM as an AI gateway on unpatched Starlette should treat this as urgent, not a routine CVE.

Coldcard Hacker Moves $7.7M More in Stolen BTC Through THORChain and CoinJoin

The attacker behind the third wave of Coldcard hardware-wallet thefts moved 97.09 BTC (~$7.7M), 45% of that wave's haul, out through THORChain on September 2 and into CoinJoin rounds over the weekend, methodically draining the largest of 293 compromised vaults first.

Why it matters — The exploit stems from a Coldcard firmware flaw disclosed in late July; Coinkite has patched it, but affected users still need to move funds to new seeds.

Bitcoin Hashrate Nears 1 Zettahash as Hashprice Jumps 22%

Bitcoin's network hashrate held near 934 EH/s, just under the 1 ZH/s threshold, while difficulty rose 1.31% to 127.45T and hashprice jumped 22% from $32.42 to $39.63 alongside bitcoin's price gains, with fees contributing under 0.5% of miner rewards.

Why it matters — Miner profitability is still almost entirely a bet on price, not transaction fees, even as the network approaches a hashrate milestone.

Bitcoin's Correlation With Gold Hits Highest Level Since 2020 as $83K Resistance Holds

As rising government bond yields rattled markets, bitcoin's 90-day correlation with gold's daily returns climbed to 0.59, the highest since the pandemic-era money-printing period, while BTC buyers turned to net selling for the first time since early June after failing to clear $83,000 resistance.

Why it matters — A rising gold correlation is often read as a sign bitcoin is trading more like a macro hedge and less like a pure risk asset.
