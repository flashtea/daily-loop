Anthropic reverses enterprise data-retention policy after customer pushback

Anthropic introduced Enterprise Frontier Safeguards, letting business customers control how their data is stored and reviewed in infrastructure they control, at no extra cost, rolling out in phases this fall. It replaces a mandatory 30-day retention policy for frontier models that Anthropic introduced in June and walked back after complaints from enterprise customers.

why it mattersRemoves a significant adoption blocker for enterprise Claude customers in finance, healthcare and law.
