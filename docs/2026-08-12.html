Anthropic to watermark all Claude-generated text and files, worldwide

Anthropic said new Claude models will embed invisible watermarks in generated text and signed C2PA provenance data in supported files, applying the marks globally rather than only in the EU, in response to transparency duties under Article 50 of the EU AI Act that took effect August 2.

why it mattersNon-compliance with Article 50 can trigger fines up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover; Anthropic says the marks persist through copy-paste and some editing.

SEC schedules August 14 vote on first formal crypto rulemaking, 'Regulation Crypto'

The SEC set an open meeting for August 14 to vote on proposing Regulation Crypto, a tailored offering regime for investment contracts involving crypto assets, building on the Digital Commodities framework that treats Bitcoin, Ether, and 14 other assets as exempt from securities law.

why it mattersIt's the first formal crypto rulemaking of Chairman Paul Atkins' tenure; a yes vote opens a public comment period rather than finalizing the rule.
